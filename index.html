DviCalculator

Smart Pricing. Simple Billing. — by Dvirukthi

DviCalculator is an Android billing app for Indian businesses such as restaurants, hotels, shops and distributors. It works fully offline: your business data is stored on your own device.

What it does

  • Customer-specific pricing – each customer can have their own price for a product, with customer-category and default prices as fallbacks, dated price changes, templates and price history.
  • Quick sales and GST invoices – CGST/SGST/IGST, PDF invoices, sharing through WhatsApp or any app you choose, returns and credit notes.
  • Payments and outstanding balances, stock, expenses and daily closing.
  • Analytics and reports – sales trends, products, customers, tax and staff reports, exportable as PDF, Excel or CSV.
  • Staff roles and PINs, with an audit log of changes.
  • Data export, import and restore using files you choose.

Optional Google Drive sync

To keep several devices of one business up to date, you can connect your own Google Drive. DviCalculator encrypts the data on your device with a passphrase only you know, then stores it in a DviCalculator Sync folder in your Drive. It asks only for access to the files it creates (drive.file); it cannot see your other Drive files, and it does not access Gmail.

Sync is optional. Without it, the app never sends your data anywhere.
